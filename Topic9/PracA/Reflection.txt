If the algorithms are different, how can the computational complexity of BFS and DFS be the same?
Answer: Although BFS and DFS explore nodes in different orders, they have the same time complexity of O(V + E) because both may need to visit every vertex and examine every edge in the worst case. The difference is their traversal order, not their asymptotic complexity.
