Contrast SortedSet and SortedDictionary. Provide an example of when each might be useful.
Answer: A sorted set must ensure that the values are unique, while a sorted dictionary must ensure that the keys are unique. SortedSet is suitable for ranking by points, while SortedDictionary is used for storing corresponding information, such as a player's wealth.
